About Me

I am a Level 3, Term 2 student pursuing the Bachelor of Urban and Regional Planning (BURP) at CUET. My work combines satellite remote sensing, spatial statistics and multi-criteria weighting (such as PCA) to assess flood, drought and waterlogging risk, and to model urban growth and land use change across Bangladesh.
